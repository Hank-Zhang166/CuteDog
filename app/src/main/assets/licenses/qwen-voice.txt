This APK contains the inference tensors used by the fixed A voice.
The build removes model components used only to encode new speaker references.
The signed APK includes the reference transcript and two small acoustic descriptors
encoded from CuteDog's original, approved synthetic Qwen VoiceDesign A voice.

qwentts.cpp and ggml are used under the MIT license; this build adjusts the
context and CPU thread limits for the mobile client's maximum phrase length
and reads the weights directly from their uncompressed APK asset ranges.
